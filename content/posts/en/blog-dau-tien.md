# 👋 Starting from a Small Website

This is a website I've been cherishing for quite a while, but only now did I find the time to truly sit down and complete it. I'm so glad I can finally write these very first lines on my own website.

I hope this will be the first of many posts to come, where I can document what I've learned, the projects I've built, and memorable experiences along my journey.

## 💡 Why Did I Build This Website?

Nowadays, building a website has become much easier, especially with the rapid development of AI tools. In the past, completing a website meant spending a fair amount of time researching technologies, designing interfaces, and implementing features one by one. Today, with just a few well-crafted prompts, AI can help us generate an initial product in no time.

This made me reflect more on the true purpose of building a website. As tools become increasingly accessible, perhaps the most important thing is no longer just *what* we can create, but *why* we want to create it and the content we bring to the table.

## ✍️ What Do I Want to Share?

This website might not have much content yet, the featured projects will be gradually added, and I still have a lot to learn myself. However, I believe we don't have to wait until everything is 100% finished to start sharing.

The content here will primarily revolve around personal projects, knowledge I explore, and lessons learned during development. Alongside that, I also want to share activities, experiences, and other topics that I find interesting.

I don't expect every post to be deeply technical or bring something entirely groundbreaking. Sometimes, a post is simply a way for me to re-explain something I just learned, document how I solved a problem, or share an experience that might be useful to others.

I truly believe that writing down what you know is a great way to learn better. When trying to articulate a problem in your own words, you get the chance to review what you've grasped, spot the missing pieces, and gain a deeper understanding of the subject.

> **Sharing is learning!**

I hope that after some time, looking back at this website, I won't just see lines of code or completed projects, but also the learning process, the experiments, the things that didn't go as planned, and my own personal growth through each stage.
